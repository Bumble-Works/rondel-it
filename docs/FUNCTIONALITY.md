# Using Rondel It

## Plan your day

Choose **Day**, **Week** or **Month** in the sidebar. Use the arrows to browse and
**Today** to return. Set your preferred first day of the week in Settings.
On a narrow window, the ☰ button opens the sidebar.

Type in **Add a task** within a day, or use the top **+** button to choose where
it belongs. Press **n** to open that composer when you aren't typing elsewhere.
Use **Tags** and **Time** before adding if needed, then press Enter or the add button.

## Track progress

Click a task's circle to move through **To do → Doing → Done**. Double-click to
finish it immediately; click a completed task's circle to reopen it. Finished
tasks settle below active work, and reopened tasks return to the top.

Choose **Cancel** in the task menu, details or review to keep a record of work
you've decided to let go. Use **Reopen task** to make it active again, or
**Delete task** to remove it.

## Edit and organize tasks

Click a task to open its details. Titles and notes save when you leave the field;
the note button on a row also opens a quick editor. While details are open, edit
that task there. Links in titles and notes open in your default browser.

Use **Flag as important** in the task menu or details to highlight a task.
**Copy to tomorrow** or **Copy to a date…** creates a separate to-do with the same
title, tags, note and flag, without its recurrence, reminder or history.

Drag the grip beside an active task to reorder it or move it between days and
containers. Dragging reorganizes work without increasing its rondel count or
leaving a history copy.

## Carry work forward

Open a task's rondel menu to choose **Tomorrow**, **Pick a date**, **Next week**,
**Next month** or **Bucket List**. Each deliberate postponement increases its
rondel count. A to-do moves to its new location; a doing task keeps its status
and leaves a read-only history copy on the day you worked on it.

**Next week** and **Next month** hold tasks in that period's **Unassigned** list
until you give them a day. Once the period starts, they appear in reviews until
resolved, including after the period ends. Starting or finishing a waiting task
places it on today. Giving it a day is scheduling and adds no rondel; pushing it
to another waiting period does.

## Keep a Bucket List

Use **Bucket List** for ideas without a date. They stay out of reviews until
you schedule them. Choose **Schedule it for…** to give one a day or waiting
period; scheduling from Bucket List adds no rondel.

## Use tags and filters

Open **Tags** on a task, in the add composer or in details to search, select or
create tags. Manage tag names and colors in Settings. Deleting a tag removes it
from all tasks.

You can also type **#name** in a task title and pick a suggestion with Enter,
Tab or a click. Picking attaches the tag and removes the typed token; unpicked
text stays in the title. Choose **Create #name** explicitly to make a new tag.

Filter by status or tags in the sidebar. **Any** matches at least one selected
tag; **All** requires every selected tag. New tasks inherit active tag filters.
Changing tags on a recurring task affects that occurrence; edit the rule in
Recurring to change the series.

## Repeat tasks

Open **Recurring** to set a title, start date, repeat pattern and optional end
date, tags, note, flag and reminder. Choose daily, monthly, yearly, a custom
interval, or the start of each week or month. Custom repeats support selected
weekdays, month dates, or a weekday such as the last Friday. Dates missing from
a month use its last day.

The first task appears immediately, even if scheduled ahead. Later occurrences
arrive when due, one at a time: finish or cancel the open occurrence before the
next can appear. Missed occurrences are skipped rather than accumulated.
Start-of-week/month tasks wait in Unassigned.

Use **Edit** on a rule to change future occurrences and its unfinished task;
completed work stays as recorded. Deleting a rule stops repetition and keeps
its existing tasks.

## Set reminders

For a task with a day, choose **Time** on its row or **Reminder** in details.
While adding a task, typing **@10:00** or **10:00** offers a reminder suggestion;
select it to use the time and remove it from the title.

Allow notifications when prompted. At the chosen time, a notification and pop-up
offer **Snooze 10 min**, **Done** or **Reschedule**. Closing the pop-up dismisses
that reminder. Changes to a recurring reminder can apply to just that task or
the whole series.

Keep the app running to receive reminders, including while its window is hidden.
Missed reminders from earlier today appear when you reopen the app. Times follow
your device's timezone.

## Review unfinished work

Set morning and end-of-day review times in **Settings → Week & day rhythm**.
The review prompts you about unfinished tasks on past days and Unassigned work
whose period has started. It also catches up a missed morning review on opening.

For each item, choose **Today**, **Next week**, **Next month**, **Pick a date**,
**Cancel** or **Done**. Finishing a past-day task records completion on its planned
day. Closing the review leaves unresolved items for the next review.

## Read reports

Open **Reports** and choose a date range and tag filters. **Custom** includes both
endpoints and allows up to one calendar year. Report weeks run Sunday–Saturday,
regardless of your board's week setting.

**Dashboard** shows progress, completions, carry-forward decisions and results
by tag. **Rondeled** counts postponements made during the selected period, so one
task can count several times. Click **Left behind** to see unfinished to-dos
still dated in the past. Collapse charts to focus the report; toggle timeline
lines or choose a calendar year where available.

**Tasks** lists each task once, combining its carry-forward history. Filter by
status, click a column heading to sort, and use **Show notes** for full notes.
**First seen** is when the task was created. **Rondels** is its lifetime count;
**Days in progress** runs from first start to completion, or today for unfinished
work, including pauses. Missing historical information can leave values blank.

## Print or export

Use **Print** to print the current board or report, or choose **Save as PDF** in
the system dialog. Board printing respects filters and omits waiting Unassigned
items. Week and Month also offer a print button for a single day.

Report printing uses the selected dates, filters and display choices. Tasks
prints every matching row in the chosen order, across all pages; **Show notes**
controls whether notes appear on paper.

In **Reports → Tasks**, **Export CSV** saves every matching row in the current
sort order, with full notes. Columns are Task, Status, Tags, First seen, Scheduled
date, Completed date, Canceled date, Days in progress, Rondels and Note. Tags are
separated by semicolons; dates use YYYY-MM-DD. Blank values mean no recorded
value. On desktop, choose where to save; the browser downloads the file.

## Sync computers

In the Mac or Windows app, choose **Settings → Sync → Choose folder…** and select
a folder inside Google Drive, OneDrive, Box or Dropbox. On the other computer,
choose the same **Rondel It** folder. Joining merges the computers' data.

Sync runs automatically after changes and while the app is open. Settings shows
its status and offers **Sync now**, **Change folder…** and **Turn off**. If the
cloud drive is unavailable, restore access and retry. Conflicting edits use the
most recent change once the files reach both computers.

Each computer keeps a local copy; the browser and desktop app have separate data.
Sync is off by default. Use a private folder: sync files are unencrypted and
readable by your cloud provider and anyone with access to that folder.

## Start at login

Enable **Settings → Week & day rhythm → Start Rondel It at login** on each Mac
or Windows computer where you want it. The app starts hidden so reminders and
reviews can arrive. Use its menu-bar or system-tray icon to open or quit it.
A red dot on the app icon means a reminder or review is open.
